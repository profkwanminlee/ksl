## Leadership

The Contemporary Korea Studies Lab is directed by the **Korea Foundation Endowed Chair Professor** in Contemporary Korean Society and New Media, housed within the Wee Kim Wee School of Communication and Information at Nanyang Technological University.

    <p>
      <strong>Kwan Min Lee (Ph.D., Stanford University)</strong> is the inaugural Korea Foundation Endowed Chair Professor at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. An elected Fellow of the International Communication Association (ICA), Prof. Lee directs the Contemporary Korea Studies Lab (KSL), the Human-AI Interaction Lab (HAIL), and the UX Lab at NTU.
    </p>
    <p>
      Previously, Lee served as Vice President and Director of User Experience (UX) at Samsung Electronics, where he founded Samsung’s Creative Lab (C-Lab) and led UX innovation for the Visual Display Division. Prior to Samsung and NTU, he was a tenured professor at the Annenberg School for Communication at the University of Southern California (USC) and founding director of the Interaction Science Research Center at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU). His research focuses on the intersection of new media technologies, human-technology interaction, Korean digital innovation, and contemporary cultural diffusion.
    </p>

## Researchers

The research community includes postdoctoral research fellows, visiting scholars, doctoral candidates, and research associates.

### Postdoctoral Fellows &amp; Visiting Scholars
- **Visiting Scholar Profile (Forthcoming):** *Senior and junior scholars from Korean and ASEAN partner universities conducting resident research at KSL.*
- **Postdoctoral Research Fellow (Recruitment Open):** *Inquiries regarding funded postdoctoral appointments in Korean digital society and new media may be directed to the Lab.*

### Graduate Student Researchers
- **Doctoral Candidates:** *Ph.D. researchers investigating topics in Korean media, digital platforms, and transnational East Asian culture.*
- **Research Assistants:** *Undergraduate and Master's research associates supporting ongoing field research and data analysis.*
