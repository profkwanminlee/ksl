## Collaboration Enquiries

The Contemporary Korea Studies Lab actively seeks to cultivate international and regional partnerships. We warmly welcome proposals from:

- **Academic Institutions &amp; University Centers:** Collaborative research projects, joint grant applications, faculty and student exchanges, and co-sponsored seminars or conferences.
- **Cultural Foundations &amp; Creative Organizations:** Inquiries concerning cultural diplomacy, Korean Wave exhibitions, film screenings, creative media roundtables, and public dialogues.
- **Governmental &amp; Policy Bodies:** Policy research briefings, bilateral Korea-Singapore and Korea-ASEAN dialogue sessions, and public forum participation.

### Submitting a Proposal

If you represent an academic institution, cultural agency, foundation, or policy organization and wish to propose a joint initiative, please include the following in your communication:

1. **Institutional Affiliation &amp; Primary Contacts**
2. **Proposed Initiative Scope** (e.g., research collaboration, workshop, visiting residency)
3. **Alignment with KSL Focus Areas** (IT innovation/digital society, Korean Wave/culture, or human exchange)
4. **Anticipated Timeline &amp; Resources**

Proposals and exploratory inquiries may be addressed directly to Prof. Kwan Min Lee at `kwanminlee@ntu.edu.sg`.
