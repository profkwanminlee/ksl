### Opportunity Profiles (Forthcoming Calls)

1. **Visiting Scholar Appointments**  
   *Open to established and emerging scholars based at Asian, European, and North American universities seeking a resident research period at NTU to work on contemporary Korean society, media, or Southeast Asian connections.*

2. **Postdoctoral Research Fellowships**  
   *Competitive postdoctoral positions supported by research grants in communication technologies, user experience, and Korean popular culture.*

3. **Graduate Research Assistantships (NTU Students)**  
   *Part-time research assistantships for NTU Master's and Ph.D. students supporting quantitative data collection, qualitative interviews, and symposium coordination.*
