{% include korean-title-underline.html %}

Official announcements, research developments, media appearances, and academic milestones from the Contemporary Korea Studies Lab at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

    <h3 class="news-headline">Contemporary Korea Studies Lab (KSL) Inaugurated at NTU Singapore</h3>
    <p class="news-summary">
      The Contemporary Korea Studies Lab has been officially inaugurated at the Wee Kim Wee School of Communication and Information (WKWSCI) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. Directed by Prof. Kwan Min Lee, the Korea Foundation Endowed Chair Professor in Contemporary Korean Society and New Media, the Lab establishes a dedicated academic hub for researching contemporary Korea and its growing connections with Singapore and the ASEAN region.
    </p>
  </article>

  <!-- News Item 2 -->
  <article class="news-item">
    <div class="news-meta">
      <span class="news-date">Academic Session 2026</span>
      <span class="news-category">Research Agenda</span>
    </div>
    <h3 class="news-headline">Tri-Pillar Research Program Announced: Digital Society, Hallyu, and Regional Exchange</h3>
    <p class="news-summary">
      KSL unveils its foundational research framework spanning three critical domains: Korea's rapid IT innovation and platform society; the transnational diffusion and creative translation of contemporary Korean popular culture across Southeast Asia; and the historical, educational, and diplomatic mobility connecting Korea with ASEAN member states.
    </p>
  </article>

  <!-- News Item 3 -->
  <article class="news-item">
    <div class="news-meta">
      <span class="news-date">Call for Papers</span>
      <span class="news-category">Special Issue</span>
    </div>
    <h3 class="news-headline">Call for Papers: "Contemporary Korea and Southeast Asia in the Platform Era"</h3>
    <p class="news-summary">
      KSL announces an international call for book chapters and journal papers addressing how digital platforms, algorithmic curation, and transnational media policies shape cultural exchange between East and Southeast Asia. Selected papers will be presented at the forthcoming KSL International Symposium at NTU.
    </p>
  </article>
