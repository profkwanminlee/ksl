## About the Korean Studies Lab

The Korean Studies Lab (KSL) is an interdisciplinary research initiative and community of scholars based at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. As one of the first initiatives of its kind in Southeast Asia, and working in connection with NTU’s Korean Studies courses, the Lab aims to expand Korean studies as a field of academic inquiry from the region and to explore contemporary Korea through digital area studies as a new methodological approach. The Lab supports empirical research, academic publications, and public programs on contemporary Korea and its growing connections with Singapore and the ASEAN region.

Building on existing scholarship and active international partnerships, the Lab examines how Korea is interpreted, translated, and engaged across diverse cultural, social, and technological contexts. With particular attention to contemporary media infrastructures, it investigates how digital platforms, algorithmic systems, and creative industries shape cultural transformation across Asia.

Through symposia, research colloquia, visiting fellowships, and joint publications, the Lab brings together scholars, students, and institutional partners across Singapore, South Korea, and Southeast Asia.

## Mission & Objectives

**Mission:** To build a hub for contemporary Korean studies in Southeast Asia that advances new ways of understanding Korea from the region.

## Institutional Context & Leadership

The Korean Studies Lab is housed within the **Wee Kim Wee School of Communication and Information (WKWSCI)** at **Nanyang Technological University, Singapore (NTU Singapore)**.

Established in connection with the **Korea Foundation Endowed Chair in Contemporary Korean Society and New Media**, the Lab operates as an open, collaborative academic space linking researchers across NTU’s College of Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences (CoHASS), the College of Computing and Data Science, and international partner universities across South Korea, Southeast Asia, and North America.
