## Academic Programs &amp; Collaborations
{% include korean-title-underline.html %}

The Korean Studies Lab hosts an active schedule of academic programs, international conferences, research collaborations, and public colloquia. These events connect scholars, graduate students, cultural practitioners, and policymakers to discuss the evolving dynamics of contemporary Korea and its ties with Southeast Asia.

    <h3 class="news-headline" style="font-size: 1.2rem; font-weight: 700; color: var(--color-ink); margin-bottom: 8px;">Korean Studies Lab (KSL) Inaugurated at NTU Singapore</h3>
    <p class="news-summary" style="font-size: 0.9rem; line-height: 1.6; color: var(--color-ink-muted); margin: 0;">
      The Korean Studies Lab has been officially inaugurated at the Wee Kim Wee School of Communication and Information (WKWSCI) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. Directed by Prof. Kwan Min Lee, the Korea Foundation Endowed Chair Professor in Contemporary Korean Society and New Media, the Lab establishes a dedicated academic hub for researching contemporary Korea and its growing connections with Singapore and the ASEAN region.
    </p>
  </article>

    <h3 class="news-headline" style="font-size: 1.2rem; font-weight: 700; color: var(--color-ink); margin-bottom: 8px;">Tri-Pillar Research Program Announced: Digital Society, Hallyu, and Regional Exchange</h3>
    <p class="news-summary" style="font-size: 0.9rem; line-height: 1.6; color: var(--color-ink-muted); margin: 0;">
      KSL unveils its foundational research framework spanning three critical domains: Korea's rapid IT innovation and platform society; the transnational diffusion and creative translation of contemporary Korean popular culture across Southeast Asia; and the historical, educational, and diplomatic mobility connecting Korea with ASEAN member states.
    </p>
