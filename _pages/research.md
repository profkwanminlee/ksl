<section id="computational-korean-studies" style="margin-bottom: 2.5rem;">
  <h2 style="font-size: 1.65rem; font-weight: 700; color: var(--color-ink); margin-bottom: 0.85rem;">
    Computational Korean Studies
  </h2>
  
  <p style="font-size: 1.02rem; line-height: 1.75; color: var(--color-ink); margin-bottom: 0;">
    We bring digital area studies into Korean studies as a new methodological approach. By combining computational and data-driven methods with established area studies scholarship, we analyse how digital platforms, algorithms, and online communities shape the circulation of Korean culture.
  </p>
</section>

<section id="comparative-korean-studies" style="margin-bottom: 2.5rem;">
  <h2 style="font-size: 1.65rem; font-weight: 700; color: var(--color-ink); margin-bottom: 0.85rem;">
    Comparative Korean Studies
  </h2>
  
  <p style="font-size: 1.02rem; line-height: 1.75; color: var(--color-ink); margin-bottom: 1.25rem;">
    We place Korea alongside Southeast Asian societies rather than within the familiar Northeast Asian frame. Developed from the Korean Studies curriculum at NTU, our comparative work examines Korea together with Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, and the Philippines across five themes: histories, states, societies, mobilities, and cultures.
  </p>
